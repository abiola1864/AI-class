Why we created this masterclass

Every week, we meet people who want to use AI but aren’t sure where to start.

A civil servant with reports to summarise. A nurse preparing a community health talk. A business owner who needs fresh content. A researcher racing a grant deadline. They need someone to show them how.

Why now

AI is already changing how businesses reach customers, how researchers find evidence and how organisations win funding. Those who learn to use it well save hours every week and spot opportunities others miss.

Our aim

To help people in every kind of work use AI with confidence on the tasks already on their desk. Not in theory, and not someday, but the very next working day.
